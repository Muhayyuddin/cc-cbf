CC-CBF: Compliant-Course Control Barrier Function

The Idea

Overview

When two ships approach each other at sea, maritime law (COLREG) specifies exactly which side each vessel must manoeuvre to. For example, in a head-on encounter both ships must turn to starboard (right) never to port. Most autonomous collision avoidance systems either avoid in any convenient direction, ignoring the rules, or follow rigid hand-coded rule tables that break down in ambiguous or multi-vessel situations. CC-CBF takes a different approach. Instead of checking a rule table and then applying a safety filter, it shapes the danger zone itself to be directionally asymmetric. The forbidden region around each obstacle is inflated on the COLREG-prohibited side and left narrow on the compliant side. A tiny quadratic program (QP) running at 20 Hz continuously projects the vessel's desired velocity onto the safe, legal side, so the right manoeuvre emerges automatically from geometry, not from rules. The key insight: if you make the barrier bigger on the wrong side, the solver's minimum-effort solution naturally steers away from it directly toward the COLREG-compliant direction without ever needing to "know" the rule.

Real-World & Simulator Experiments

Validation on Physical USVs & MBZIRC Simulator

CC-CBF was validated beyond simulation: the algorithm was deployed on a real unmanned surface vessel and tested in the MBZIRC maritime simulator. Both head-on (Rule 14) and overtaking (Rule 13) encounters are demonstrated, confirming that the same controller generalises from simulation to physical hardware without re-tuning.

Results

Monte Carlo Evaluation

50 randomised trials × 4 scenarios × 5 controllers = 1 000 simulation runs. Initial conditions perturbed with Gaussian noise on position (σ = 5 m), speed (σ = 0.3 m/s) and heading (σ = 5°).

Controller Collision % Min Sep (m) COLREG Compliance Path Efficiency
CC-CBF Ours 0.0 % 13.0 0.99 0.98
C3BF 0.0 % 18.5 0.38 0.94
Rule-COLREG 2.5 % 9.9 0.96 1.00
Geo-CRI 9.0 % 8.4 0.65 1.02
TC-CBF 1.0 % 10.5 0.87 0.86

CC-CBF is the only controller with zero collisions across all 1 000 trials and achieves the highest COLREG compliance. C3BF also avoids collisions but at the cost of nearly zero directional compliance (0.38).
